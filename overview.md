# Bacara Club — Client Overview

## Who they are
Bacara is a nightclub located at **235 23rd St, Miami Beach, FL**. It operates Wednesday through Saturday, from roughly 11:30 PM – 5:00 AM, and is known for electric energy, top-tier DJs, and a luxe, vibrant atmosphere. Bacara was recently acquired by **Clavicular** (reported as a $10M+ investment), who is repositioning the venue as a streamer-first, always-on broadcast nightclub.

## The new direction (under Clavicular)
Bacara is being rebuilt around a simple, defensible positioning: **the first nightclub built for the stream**. Unlike traditional Miami clubs that have started cracking down on filming, Bacara encourages it. Cameras, creators, and livestream audiences are treated as part of the room — not a liability. Expect permanent stream rigs, creator-friendly lighting, dedicated streamer tables, and integrated social/chat overlays that turn every night into a broadcast event.

The flagship nights are **Wednesday** and **Saturday** — these are the two pillars the entire marketing, SEO, and ads strategy should be built around. Wednesdays own the mid-week Miami Beach nightlife conversation; Saturdays are the main-event, biggest-room-of-the-week moment.

## Core offering
- Table reservations and bottle service (primary revenue)
- Guest list / general admission
- Private events and buyouts
- Streamer partnerships (creator tables, collab nights, livestreams to IG/TikTok/Kick/Twitch)
- Weekly recurring programming anchored on **Wednesdays** and **Saturdays**

## Audience segments
1. **Table buyers / bottle service crowd** — high-ticket, primary revenue driver. Decision window is short (same-week, often same-day).
2. **Streamers / creators / their audiences** — the differentiator. Drives organic reach and makes Bacara appointment-viewing.
3. **Guest list / GA crowd** — fills the room, creates the energy, becomes UGC.
4. **Tourists & hotel concierges** — search "best Miami Beach clubs," "Wednesday night Miami," "Saturday night Miami Beach club."
5. **Private event buyers** — corporate, birthdays, artist afterparties, buyouts.

## Positioning statement
> Bacara is Miami Beach's first streaming nightclub — a luxe, always-on broadcast venue where Wednesday and Saturday nights are engineered to be seen, shared, and streamed. Backed by Clavicular, built for creators, open to the world.
